# ZCL Member Clusters

The clusters of the ZCL are divided into functional areas, for convenience. An application can use clusters from any number of these areas to make up its complete functionality. The clusters implemented by NXP are from the following areas \(the associated clusters are listed in the referenced sub-sections\):

-   General - [Section 1.1.1](general.md#id_4aa6c180-7aec-4db8-9b35-724258df3130)

-   Measurement and Sensing - [Section 1.1.2](measurement_and_sensing.md#id_93ea2846-a2c8-4a7e-aa2f-5d6f3d233b76)

-   Lighting - [Section 1.1.3](lighting.md#id_0a8e17c3-dc5f-4bef-a5ac-a1d635d10d97)

-   Heating, Ventilation and Air-Conditioning \(HVAC\) - [Section 1.1.4](heating_ventilation_and_air-conditioning_hvac.md#id_5752a31d-8311-41de-8665-fc82b2352ead)

-   Closures - [Section 1.1.5](closures.md#id_8eddce64-7f7d-4459-ba84-82d4dca54772)

-   Security and Safety - [Section 1.1.6](security_and_safety.md#id_46495614-edff-474b-82e3-fb2aef232bc9)

-   Smart Energy - [Section 1.1.7](smart_energy.md#id_e42427bf-d756-4f62-844b-5f3bd7b5d9b5)

-   Commissioning - [Section 1.1.8](commissioning.md#id_13771fe8-71e3-428b-9751-051fc9400313)

-   Appliances - [Section 1.1.9](appliances.md#id_0261396c-2326-43ba-9ac5-ac4509c62cd4)

-   Over-The-Air \(OTA\) Upgrade - [Section 1.1.10](over-the-air_ota_upgrade.md#id_1f5444de-c30f-4f91-a90e-488dbd6dd9da)


**Note:** Not all of the clusters from the above ZCL functional areas are available in the NXP software.
